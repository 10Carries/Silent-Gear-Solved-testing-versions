How the numbers — and the alloy rules — are calculated

Per‑material values. Each cell in your file is read as one or more tokens: a plain number averages in as a base value; +N adds flat; N% with a sign scales the running total; a bare N% averages in as a fraction; xN multiplies the total; a trailing Nx averages in as a multiplier baseline; and name (N) (used for Rarity / Harvest Tier) reads the number in parentheses.

Which materials can alloy together. A material can be a Coating, Grip, Main, etc. depending on which rows it has in your file. Two or more materials can only be alloyed if they share at least one of those roles (in‑game logic, recreated from AlloyMakerBlockEntity), and a shared role is dropped if every material in the recipe is flagged "Additive" for it. Whatever roles survive are the only ones the resulting alloy keeps — so combining Iron and Gold keeps whatever they both do, but drops "Coating" since Iron can't be one.

Combining an alloy. For every surviving shared role, every material's token for that role is pooled, in the order the materials were chosen. Whichever material comes first sets the "primary" baseline that weights every other averaged token — so a different pick for the first slot changes the result, same as in‑game. Materials after the first are treated as an unordered group, matching how the weighting math behaves for anything past the primary slot. Harvest Tier is the one exception — it isn't averaged at all, it simply takes the highest tier among the materials used.

Synergy. Recreated from the mod's own formula: a bonus for using more distinct materials, a penalty if the parts share no common category, a small bonus for categories shared by more than one part, and a small penalty scaled by how far each part's Rarity sits from the first part's Rarity. Synergy is clamped between 0.1x and 2.0x, and isn't applied to Harvest Tier. Traits are shown/filterable for reference but their exact mechanical effects aren't factored into the math, since that logic isn't data‑driven in the export.

Which station makes it. Recipes made only from Metal and/or Dust materials can go in the Alloy Forge / Metal Alloyer. Anything else needs the Super Mixer (added in version 4.0.2). Both stations are slot‑limited, and slot counts increase in version 4.1.5 — set your version above to match your world.

Ranking. For every stat you selected, every material/alloy gets a rank — highest final value = rank 1. Ranks across all your selected stats are averaged, and the final list is sorted with the lowest average rank (i.e. the best overall) first.

Auto‑narrowing the alloy search. Order‑sensitive combinations with repeats allowed grow explosively, so instead of asking you to hand‑pick materials, the tool scores every eligible material by how strong it is, on its own, across your selected stats, then automatically keeps as many of the top scorers as will fit inside a combination budget for your chosen max alloy size (whitelisted materials are always kept, regardless of score). This is a heuristic, not an exhaustive guarantee of the single best possible alloy — but it gets you dramatically closer than manual testing, in well under a minute.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing you upload leaves your machine.
