How the numbers are calculated

Per‑material values. Each cell in your file is read as one or more tokens: a plain number averages in as a base value; +N adds flat; N% with a sign scales the running total; a bare N% averages in as a fraction; xN multiplies the total; a trailing Nx averages in as a multiplier baseline; and name (N) (used for Rarity / Harvest Tier) reads the number in parentheses.

Combining an alloy. All tokens from every chosen part are pooled in the order the parts were chosen. Whichever part comes first sets the "primary" baseline value, which weights every other averaged token — so putting a different part first can change the result, exactly like in‑game. Everything after the first part is treated as an unordered group, matching how the weighting math behaves for anything past the primary slot.

Synergy. Recreated from the mod's own formula: a bonus for using more distinct parts, a penalty if the parts share no common category, a small bonus for categories shared by more than one part, and a small penalty scaled by how far each part's Rarity sits from the first part's Rarity. Synergy is clamped between 0.1x and 2.0x. Traits are shown for reference but aren't factored into these numbers, since their effects aren't data‑driven in the export.

Ranking. For every stat you selected, every material/alloy gets a rank — lowest raw value = rank 1. Ranks across all your selected stats are averaged, and the final list is sorted with the lowest average rank first.

Why the alloy tab limits your selection. Order‑sensitive combinations with repeats allowed grow explosively (imagine picking a first part, then any 5 more from the same list). To keep the browser responsive, narrow the candidate list and/or the max alloy size until the combination count fits inside the budget shown above the Compute button.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing you upload leaves your machine.
